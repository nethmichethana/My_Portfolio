# 🌐 Personal Portfolio Website

Welcome to my personal portfolio! This project showcases my professional profile, technical skills, projects, and career journey in the field of Information Technology.

The portfolio is designed with a clean, modern, and user-friendly interface to present my work, highlight my technical capabilities, and provide an easy way to connect with me professionally.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Introduction** – An overview of my background and career interests.
- **About Me** – Information about my professional journey and aspirations.
- **Technical Skills** – Technologies, tools, and skills I have developed.
- **Projects Showcase** – A collection of projects demonstrating my practical knowledge and development experience.
- **Contact Section** – A convenient way to connect with me.
